FIELD NOTE / RISK MANAGEMENT

Threat Modeling, Risk Scoring & Remediation

Three linked exercises in application threat modeling, vulnerability assessment and security-control prioritization.

Threat modeling

In a simulated sneaker-commerce application, I used a PASTA worksheet to identify business requirements (including transaction security), describe the technical scope and consider injection and session hijacking risks.

Vulnerability assessment

For a hypothetical Linux/MySQL system, I evaluated access-control risks and recommended authentication, authorization, audit logging, MFA, role-based access control, TLS and IP allow-listing.

Risk scoring

A banking scenario used a simple likelihood-by-severity matrix. A public-facing backup database was scored highest among the supplied sample risks (9 out of 9), highlighting the need to prioritize high-impact information exposure.

Least privilege

A separate data-leak exercise applied NIST SP 800-53 AC-6 to inappropriate folder sharing and recommended restricting access by role and regularly auditing privileges.

Based on the PASTA worksheet, vulnerability assessment, risk register and data-handling exercise. All business contexts were hypothetical training scenarios.
